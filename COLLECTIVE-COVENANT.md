# The Collective Covenant

**The Collective** is built on a simple principle: *what we learn should carry forward, while every company keeps full ownership of its own records, code, and authority.*

## 1. Sovereign Records & Local Authority

- Your company's general ledger, customer records, operational databases, and institutional memory stay inside your own estate.
- Model inference is routed only through your approved enterprise or local boundaries.
- No raw language model ever writes directly to a book of record. Every workflow block runs under a named human (`Operator`) holding runtime authority, with deterministic code validating preconditions and invariants outside the model session.

## 2. How a Pattern Travels

What travels across The Collective, when a member chooses to share it, is the stripped, battle-tested **Workflow Block (`wfb.package.v1`)**:

1. **Zero Proprietary Data:** No company-specific account numbers, vendor names, customer records, pricing, or internal credentials are ever included in a shared pattern.
2. **Deterministic Invariants Included:** Every shared pattern includes its JSON Schema contract, precondition checks, double-entry or operational invariants, and negative test fixtures so the next builder can verify it in one command.
3. **Human Command Preserved:** Every block explicitly names the human role responsible for exception authority and runtime sign-off.

## 3. Direct Peer Exchange

The Collective is not a public forum or a marketing list. It is a working exchange among enterprise leaders, operators, and builders who have lived the gap between recorded state in the books and work as lived on the floor.
